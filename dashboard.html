Add an expense

Running costs that are not paper or supplier bills: toner, service, electricity, rent, salary. These reduce net profit.
Two-monthly electricity bill: enter the bill amount and “2” months — it is split across this month and last month. Rent: tick “Repeat every month” and it is added by itself on the 1st of each month. Bill not paid yet? Enter its due date and untick “Already paid” — it shows as due until you press “Mark paid”.
